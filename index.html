How this works

Claude checks the student's admissions email every hour and reads the Common App dashboard and any signed-in school portal twice a day, then updates this page. New schools show up within an hour of their application confirmation. Urgent news is texted to the family right away; anything that comes in between 10 PM and 7 AM is texted at 7 AM. Nothing is ever submitted, changed or paid for without Mom's OK.

Email buttons open a message to the school with the student's name and ID numbers already filled in; add your question and send it from your own email. Call buttons dial the admissions office.

Urgent: something is missing or a deadline is here. To do: a submission or decision is waiting on the family. To verify: the school's own checklist still needs a look. Done: the school says the file is complete. School badges use initials and colors, not official logos.
